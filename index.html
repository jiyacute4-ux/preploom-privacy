Privacy Policy — Preploom JazuTool

Effective date: 27 November 2026
App: Preploom JazuTool  ·  Owner: Preploom (Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/Preploom)

1. What this app is

Preploom JazuTool is a personal, single-user tool. It connects the owner's own Pinterest business account to a small app running on the owner's own computer, so the owner can create and schedule Pinterest pins for their own Etsy shop products.

2. Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Pinterest OAuth), the app accesses only the owner's own Pinterest data: their account information, their boards, and their pins. It does not access any other person's account or data.

3. Data the app stores

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer (in the app's local configuration and local database). Pin drafts, pin schedules, and approval records are also stored only on the owner's own computer. No personal data is sent to any server other than Pinterest itself, and only to carry out actions the owner approved — creating or scheduling a pin, or reading the owner's own boards.

4. Data the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect data about anyone else. It uses no tracking, no analytics, and no cookies. It does not sell, share, or disclose any data to any third party. Pins are published only after the owner reviews and approves each one.

5. Your choices

The app has a single user — its owner. The owner can disconnect the app at any time from their Pinterest account settings, which revokes its access. Tokens stored on the owner's computer can be deleted from the app at any time.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
